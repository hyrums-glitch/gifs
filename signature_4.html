Golfsheds Email Signature

About 2 minutes. Do this on a computer, not your phone.

1Enter your info

2Copy your signature

3Paste it into Gmail

  1. Open Gmail signature settings (opens in a new tab):
    https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/#settings/general
    Open Gmail Settings →
  2. Scroll down to Signature and click + Create new. Name it Golfsheds.
  3. Click in the big signature box and paste: Cmd + V on Mac, Ctrl + V on PC.
  4. Under Signature defaults, set both For new emails use and On reply/forward use to Golfsheds.
  5. Check "Insert signature before quoted text in replies…" so it shows on replies.
  6. Scroll all the way down and click Save Changes.
  7. Send a test email to yourself to check it.

?If something looks off

Images show as broken boxes or are missing: refresh Gmail. If they're still missing, delete the signature, come back here, click Copy again, and repaste. Don't copy from an old email.

Logo lands above the text instead of beside it: delete everything in the signature box, click in the empty box, and paste again. Don't paste into a box that still has an old signature in it. If it still stacks, tell Hyrum which browser you used.

Signature shows as plain text with no images: in a new email, click the ⋮ menu (bottom right) and turn off Plain text mode.

Copy button didn't work: use Chrome on a computer and try again. Don't copy the preview by hand, because those images won't carry over into Gmail.

Gmail phone app: the phone app uses its own text-only signature. Set it in the app under Settings → your account → Signature settings, using: Your Name | Golfsheds | golfsheds.com
